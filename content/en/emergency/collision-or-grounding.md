## Situation

You have hit another vessel, a rock, a wreck or the bottom, or another
vessel has hit you.

## Do now

1. **Stop and shout.** Count everyone. Lifejackets on
    ([MCA, p. 82](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).
2. **Look after the injured** and anyone trapped
    ([MCA, p. 83](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).
3. **Check for water coming in**: bilges, forepeak, every locker below the
    waterline. Any water at all: go to
    [Taking on water](taking-on-water.html)
    ([MCA, p. 83](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).
4. **Collision:** ask the other boat if they need help, and give it if
    you can ([MCA, p. 83](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).
5. **Call the coastguard** by DSC and VHF if you need help; send a
    [Mayday](mayday-distress-call.html) if lives are at risk
    ([MCA, p. 83](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).
6. **Aground:**
    - Do not power off hard before you know the hull is sound.
    - Lay out an anchor towards deep water so the wind and sea do not push
      you further on ([MCA, p. 84](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).
    - To float off, take weight off: people, water, gear
      ([MCA, p. 84](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).
    - If you cannot refloat, or the damage is too great, stay where you
      are and get help ([MCA, p. 84](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).
7. **Get the liferaft ready.** Plan a route to shelter if you can move
    ([MCA, p. 83](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).

## Why

A hole may be hidden below the floor or behind lockers, and any leak is
serious ([FM 55-501, 22-1](../library/fm-55-501-marine-crewman.html)). A
boat aground can fall over as the tide drops
([Boat Crew Handbook, p. 4-6](../library/uscg-boat-crew-handbook-seamanship.html)),
so watch the heel and keep people ready to get off.

## Check that it worked

Everyone is accounted for, no water is coming in (or the pumps are
winning), and the coastguard knows where you are.

## If that fails

- Water gaining: [Taking on water](taking-on-water.html), then
  [Abandon ship](abandon-ship.html).
- Aground on a falling tide: see
  [Tides and tidal streams](../run-the-boat/tides-and-tidal-streams.html)
  for when the water comes back.
