## Situation

Water is rising in the bilge or over the cabin sole, a bilge alarm sounds,
or the boat feels heavy and sluggish.

## Do now

1. **Raise the alarm. Lifejackets on everyone**
    ([MCA, p. 81](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).
2. **Start every pump**: electric, manual, buckets
    ([MCA, p. 82](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).
3. **Call the coastguard now** (DSC and VHF), before you start hunting
    for the leak; with two or more aboard, one person does this while
    the others pump ([MCA, p. 81](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).
    If the pumps are not keeping up, send a
    **[Mayday](mayday-distress-call.html)**.
4. **Find where it comes in.** Look and feel along the seacocks, their
    hoses and other pipework, the shaft seal, then the hull itself. Check
    the forepeak and tanks too
    ([MCA, pp. 80 to 81](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).
5. **Close the seacocks** you can reach, starting with any that leaks
    ([MCA, p. 83](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).
6. **Plug the hole.** Drive in a soft wooden plug. Or stuff in rags,
    cushions, a mattress, clothing; hold it with a board, a bucket, your
    foot. Cutting the flow by half is a good job
    ([FM 55-501, 22-5, 22-26 to 22-35](../library/fm-55-501-marine-crewman.html)).
7. **Switch off electrics** in the flooded area
    ([MCA, p. 83](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).
8. **Keep the coastguard told** how fast the water is coming in. Pumps
    losing: **[Mayday](mayday-distress-call.html)** if not sent already.
9. **Get the liferaft and grab bag ready**
    ([MCA, p. 82](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).

## Why

There is no such thing as a little leak
([FM 55-501, 22-1](../library/fm-55-501-marine-crewman.html)). The deeper
and bigger the hole, the faster the water comes in, so plug it at once
([FM 55-501, 22-5](../library/fm-55-501-marine-crewman.html)). Water
sloshing inside makes the boat less stable and can roll it over
([Boat Crew Handbook, p. 4-9](../library/uscg-boat-crew-handbook-seamanship.html)).

## Check that it worked

The water level stops rising, then falls. Mark the level (a pencil line,
a ruler) and check it every few minutes.

## If that fails

- Water still rising: keep pumping, keep the coastguard told, head for
  shelter or shallow water if it is safe
  ([MCA, p. 83](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).
- Boat going down: [Abandon ship](abandon-ship.html).
- Later: [Bilge pump not working](../fix-it/bilge-pump-not-working.html),
  [Leaking stern gland](../fix-it/leaking-stern-gland.html).
