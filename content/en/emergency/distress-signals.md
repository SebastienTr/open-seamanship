## Situation

You need to tell other boats, aircraft or people ashore that you are in
grave danger and need help, by radio, light, smoke or sound.

## Do now

1. **Radio first:** DSC distress button and [Mayday](mayday-distress-call.html);
    switch on the EPIRB or PLB.
2. **Get your flares out and read the instructions now**, before a ship
    is in sight ([FM 55-501, p. 10-35](../library/fm-55-501-marine-crewman.html)).
3. **Fire only when you see or hear a ship or aircraft.** Rockets for a
    ship far off; hand flares when they are close or overhead; orange smoke
    by day only ([FM 55-501, p. 10-35](../library/fm-55-501-marine-crewman.html)).
4. **Hold a hand flare downwind**, on the lee side, at arm's length, over
    the water, fingers below the top. Never point it at anyone ([FM 4-01.502, 2-64](../library/fm-4-01-502-watercraft-safety.html);
    [FM 55-501, p. 10-35](../library/fm-55-501-marine-crewman.html)).
5. **By day, flash a mirror** round the horizon
    ([FM 55-501, 10-93](../library/fm-55-501-marine-crewman.html)).
6. **No flares?** Raise and lower both arms slowly, sound the horn
    continuously, flash SOS with a torch.

## Why

These signals mean "I am in distress and need immediate help". Using them
for anything else is forbidden
([Pub. 102, p. 139](../library/nga-pub102.html)).

| Signal | How |
| --- | --- |
| **MAYDAY** | Spoken by radio |
| DSC distress alert | Red button, VHF channel 70 or MF/HF DSC |
| EPIRB, PLB, SART | Beacon switched on |
| **SOS** | ··· – – – ··· by light or sound |
| Red rocket, parachute flare or hand flare | Fired one at a time |
| Orange smoke | Canister, by day |
| Arms | Raised and lowered slowly, both sides |
| Fog horn | Sounded continuously |
| Gun or explosion | About once a minute |
| Flags **N** over **C** | Code flags |
| Square flag with a ball above or below it | Any square shape and ball |
| Flames on the boat | Burning tar or oil barrel |
| Orange sheet with black square and circle; dye in the sea | Seen from the air |

([Pub. 102, p. 139](../library/nga-pub102.html);
[Wikipedia, 'Distress signal'](../library/wikipedia-en.html)).

## Check that it worked

Rescuers reply with **orange smoke**, or **three white star signals about
a minute apart**: "You are seen; help is coming"
([Pub. 102, p. 141](../library/nga-pub102.html)).

## If that fails

Keep signalling each time a ship or aircraft comes into view. Do not
waste flares or smoke on ships that cannot see you
([FM 55-501, p. 10-35](../library/fm-55-501-marine-crewman.html)). Never fire out-of-date flares for practice
([Wikipedia](../library/wikipedia-en.html)). See also
[Signal flags](../tables/signal-flags.html) and
[GMDSS and DSC](../run-the-boat/gmdss-and-dsc.html).
