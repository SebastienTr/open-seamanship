## Situation

The boat is sinking, burning or breaking up and cannot be saved. The
skipper gives the order to abandon ship
([MCA, p. 88](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).

## Do now

1. **[Mayday](mayday-distress-call.html)**: give position, number of people,
    injuries, and **"abandoning to liferaft"**
    ([MCA, p. 87](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).
2. **Dress warm:** extra layers, waterproofs, hat; immersion suit if you
    have one. **Lifejacket on and done up**
    ([FM 55-501, 10-19](../library/fm-55-501-marine-crewman.html)).
3. **Take:** grab bag, EPIRB, PLBs, handheld VHF, flares, water,
    seasickness tablets ([MCA, p. 88](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).
4. **Launch the liferaft:** check the painter is tied to the boat, put the
    raft over the side, pull the painter all the way out until it inflates
    ([MCA, p. 85](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).
5. **Board it dry** from the deck, a ladder or a rope. Never jump onto the
    canopy ([FM 55-501, 10-88](../library/fm-55-501-marine-crewman.html)).
6. **If you must jump:** not from higher than 4.5 m (15 ft). Check below,
    arms crossed holding the lifejacket, one hand over nose and mouth, feet
    together, look ahead, jump feet first
    ([MCA, pp. 88 to 89](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).
7. **In the water:** go to the raft or float still. Do not swim
    aimlessly. Keep together
    ([MCA, p. 89](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).
8. **When everyone is in,** cut the painter (knife by the entrance). Get
    clear of the boat but stay near the spot
    ([MCA, pp. 88 to 89](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).
9. **Switch on the EPIRB** and tie it to the raft. Stream the drogue, close
    the entrance, bail, take seasickness tablets, post a lookout
    ([MCA, pp. 89 to 90](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).

## Why

People who get into a liferaft, and people in immersion suits, survive far
more often ([NIOSH](../library/niosh-survive-sinking.html)). Wet clothes
still slow heat loss; swimming speeds it up
([MCA, p. 89](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).

## Check that it worked

Everyone is counted and in the raft, the EPIRB light is flashing, the
entrance is closed.

## If that fails

- Raft upside down: pull it over by the righting strap, then move back
  ([MCA, p. 85](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).
- No raft: stay with anything that floats, huddle together. See
  [Person in cold water](person-in-cold-water.html).
