## Situation

Your dinghy, kayak, skiff, RIB or small open boat has turned over, or is
full of water, and you are in the water beside it.

## Do now

1. **Count heads.** Shout each name. Anyone missing may be under the hull.
2. **Trapped underneath?** There is often air under the hull: breathe,
    then pull yourself out along the side, **face up**, so your lifejacket
    does not catch ([FM 55-501, 10-95](../library/fm-55-501-marine-crewman.html)).
3. **Stay with the boat.** Hold on to it or climb onto the hull. It floats,
    it is far easier to see than a head in the water, and it gets you partly
    out of the cold ([FM 55-501, 10-49, 10-62](../library/fm-55-501-marine-crewman.html)).
4. **Do not swim for shore** unless it is very close. Swimming burns heat
    and strength fast ([MCA, pp. 75, 89](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html);
    [FM 21-22, p. 48](../library/fm-21-22-survival-at-sea-1950.html)).
5. **Lifejackets done up**, cuffs and collars tight, keep your clothes on
    ([MCA, p. 75](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).
6. **Call for help:** PLB with the aerial out of the water, handheld VHF
    ([Mayday](mayday-distress-call.html)), whistle, flares, waving arms
    ([MCA leaflet](../library/mca-fishing-leaflets.html);
    [Distress signals](distress-signals.html)).
7. **Get out of the water as far as you can.** In the water: knees up to
    the chest, or huddle close together
    ([FM 55-501, 10-47, 10-48](../library/fm-55-501-marine-crewman.html)).
8. **Dinghy built to be righted?** Stand on the centreboard, lean back,
    bail it out ([Wikipedia, 'Capsizing'](../library/wikipedia-en.html)).
    Do not wear yourself out on a boat that will not come up.

## Why

You lose heat about 25 times faster in water than in air
([FM 55-501, 10-49](../library/fm-55-501-marine-crewman.html)). In the
first minutes, cold shock makes you gasp; without a lifejacket it can kill
in under five minutes ([MCA leaflet](../library/mca-fishing-leaflets.html)).

## Check that it worked

Everyone counted, holding on or on the hull, help called.

## If that fails

- Boat sinking: stay together, float still in your lifejackets. See
  [Person in cold water](person-in-cold-water.html).
- After rescue: [Hypothermia](../health/hypothermia.html).
