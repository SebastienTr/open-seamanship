## Situation

The engine has stopped or will not drive the boat, and wind, waves or tide
are carrying you towards rocks, a beach, shallows or a harbour wall.

## Do now

1. **Look and judge.** How far to danger? How fast are you drifting?
    That is how much time you have.
2. **Anchor** if the water is shallow enough. Let out all the rode you can:
    at least 5 times the depth, 10 times in heavy weather
    ([Boat Crew Handbook, 6-128](../library/uscg-boat-crew-handbook-seamanship.html);
    [MCA, p. 84](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).
    Even a dragging anchor slows you down.
3. **Sailing boat:** get sail up and sail away from the danger.
4. **Lifejackets on everyone.**
5. **Call for help early.**
    - Time to spare: **Pan-Pan** (DSC urgency, then voice on channel 16)
      ([MCA](../library/mca-gmdss-small-boat-procedures.html)).
    - You will hit before help arrives, or people are at risk:
      **[Mayday](mayday-distress-call.html)**
      ([FM 55-501, 9-71](../library/fm-55-501-marine-crewman.html)).
6. **Quick checks**, only once the drift is under control:
    - Rope or net round the propeller? Look over the stern
      ([Wikipedia](../library/wikipedia-en.html)).
    - Fuel tap open, tank not empty; outboard kill cord in place.
7. **Get ready to be towed**: a strong line ready at the bow. See
    [Towing and being towed](../run-the-boat/towing.html).

## Why

Rocks and surf break boats in minutes; finding the fault can take an hour.
Holding position first buys time. The coastguard takes engine failure
calls on the distress channel and moves you to a working channel if you
are not in immediate danger
([FM 55-501, 9-96 to 9-97](../library/fm-55-501-marine-crewman.html)).

## Check that it worked

The distance to danger stops shrinking (watch a fixed mark on shore, or the
GPS), and someone is coming.

## If that fails

- Still drifting on: let out more rode, add a second anchor, send Mayday.
- About to hit: everyone on deck with lifejackets on, away from the
  impact point; see [Collision or grounding](collision-or-grounding.html).
- Find the fault: [Engine stops or loses power](../fix-it/engine-stops-or-loses-power.html),
  [Rope round the propeller](../fix-it/fouled-propeller.html),
  [Outboard won't start](../fix-it/outboard-wont-start.html),
  [Bleeding a diesel](../fix-it/bleeding-a-diesel.html).
