## Situation

Someone has gone over the side, or is missing and may have. Every second
counts: they drift away fast and are very hard to see.

## Do now

1. **Shout "Man overboard, port side!"** (or starboard, bow, stern). Keep
    shouting until everyone knows
    ([FM 55-501, 10-51](../library/fm-55-501-marine-crewman.html)).
2. **At the helm, at once: keep the propeller away from them.** Swing the
    stern away, or go to neutral if they are close. This happens at the
    same time as the next steps, not after them
    ([FM 55-501, 10-53](../library/fm-55-501-marine-crewman.html)).
3. **Throw a lifebuoy** as close to them as you can, with its light at
    night. Then anything else that floats
    ([MCA, p. 74](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).
4. **One person points** at them and does nothing else. Never lose sight
    ([FM 55-501, 10-56](../library/fm-55-501-marine-crewman.html)).
5. **Press MOB** on the GPS or plotter to save the position
    ([MCA, p. 74](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).
6. **DSC alert and Mayday**: see [Mayday](mayday-distress-call.html)
    ([MCA, p. 74](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).
7. **Turn back.**
    - Under power: check for ropes in the water first, then turn and come
      back slowly ([Wikipedia](../library/wikipedia-en.html)). A ship can
      use a Williamson turn
      ([FM 55-501, 10-58](../library/fm-55-501-marine-crewman.html)).
    - Under sail: tack at once without freeing the jib (quick stop), then
      come back slowly, or start the engine
      ([Wikipedia](../library/wikipedia-en.html)).
8. **Stop alongside them**, engine in neutral. Throw a line; attach them to
    the boat at once ([Wikipedia](../library/wikipedia-en.html)).
9. **Lift them out lying flat** if you can, not upright
    ([Wikipedia](../library/wikipedia-en.html)). Rescuers wear lifejackets
    and are clipped on. Nobody jumps in unless there is no other way, and
    then only on a line
    ([MCA, p. 73](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).

## Why

Cold water and drifting kill quickly. A person in the water is in grave
and imminent danger, so call Mayday, not Pan-Pan
([Wikipedia](../library/wikipedia-en.html)). The person who saw the fall
is the best lookout ([FM 55-501, 10-56](../library/fm-55-501-marine-crewman.html)).

## Check that it worked

The person is aboard, breathing, lying down and out of the wind. Then
treat for cold: [Person in cold water](person-in-cold-water.html),
[Hypothermia](../health/hypothermia.html).

## If that fails

- Lost sight: steer back to the MOB position and post more lookouts. Tell
  the rescue centre; at night use a searchlight
  ([FM 55-501, 10-55](../library/fm-55-501-marine-crewman.html)).
- Cannot lift them: use a sling, a loop of rope under the arms, a tackle
  or the liferaft ([Wikipedia](../library/wikipedia-en.html)).
- Not breathing: [Near drowning](../health/near-drowning.html).
