## Situation

You see smoke or flames, smell burning, or a fire alarm sounds. Fire on a
boat spreads in minutes and the smoke kills first.

## Do now

1. **Shout "FIRE!"** and say where. Raise the alarm before you fight it
    ([MCA, p. 77](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html);
    [FM 4-01.502, 3-35](../library/fm-4-01-502-watercraft-safety.html)).
2. **Get everyone out of the cabin** onto the open deck, upwind of the
    smoke. Count them. In smoke, crawl
    ([MCA, p. 77](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).
3. **Cut what feeds it:**
    - **Galley pan:** cooker off, fire blanket over it, leave it at least
      5 minutes ([MCA, p. 77](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).
    - **Electrical:** battery switch off, then use CO2 or powder
      ([FM 4-01.502, 3-9](../library/fm-4-01-502-watercraft-safety.html)).
    - **Fuel or gas:** close the fuel or gas valve. Do not put out a gas
      flame until the gas is off
      ([FM 4-01.502, 3-9, 3-31](../library/fm-4-01-502-watercraft-safety.html)).
4. **Lifejackets on. DSC alert and [Mayday](mayday-distress-call.html)**
    unless the fire is already out. Do not wait to see if you can fight
    it: with two or more aboard, one person calls while the others fight
    the fire ([MCA, p. 78](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).
5. **Small fire and a clear way out behind you?** Fight it from upwind.
    Aim at the **base** of the flames nearest you and sweep side to side
    ([FM 55-501, 11-105, 11-117](../library/fm-55-501-marine-crewman.html)).
6. **Close hatches, doors and vents** to starve it of air
    ([MCA, p. 77](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)). Do not
    open a hot hatch or door to look: feel it with the back of your hand
    ([FM 4-01.502, 3-37](../library/fm-4-01-502-watercraft-safety.html)).
7. **Engine-room fire:** stop the engine and fans, close the space. If a
    fixed system sounds its alarm, get out and shut the door
    ([MCA, pp. 77 to 78](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).
8. **Get the liferaft ready**, away from heat and smoke
    ([MCA, p. 78](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)).

## Why

Fire needs fuel, heat and air; take away one and it dies
([FM 4-01.502, 3-2](../library/fm-4-01-502-watercraft-safety.html)).
Most people who die in fires are killed by smoke and lack of oxygen, not
burns ([FM 4-01.502, 3-48](../library/fm-4-01-502-watercraft-safety.html)).

## Check that it worked

No flames, no new smoke, surfaces cooling. Keep someone watching it with
an extinguisher ready: powder does not cool, so the fire can flare up
again ([FM 55-501, 11-118](../library/fm-55-501-marine-crewman.html);
[MCA, p. 77](../library/mca-fishermens-safety-guide.html)). Check the
spaces next to it.

## If that fails

Fire growing, extinguishers empty, or smoke filling the boat: send
Mayday if not done, and [abandon ship](abandon-ship.html). Burns: see
[Burns](../health/burns.html).
