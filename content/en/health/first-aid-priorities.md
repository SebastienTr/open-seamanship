## Situation

Someone on board is hurt, has collapsed, or is suddenly very ill. You need an
order of work so that the thing that kills first is dealt with first.

## Do now

1. **Make it safe.** Check for danger to you first, then to the casualty:
    moving gear, fire, fumes, live wiring, the edge of the deck
    ([Hospital Corpsman, p. 4-3](../library/navedtra-14295-hospital-corpsman.html)).
    Do not enter a space with fumes or low oxygen without breathing apparatus
    (see [Carbon monoxide poisoning](carbon-monoxide.html)). Slow or heave to
    if that helps.
2. **Shout for help.** Get one person to the radio and one to fetch the
    first-aid kit.
3. **Stop severe bleeding** with firm direct pressure. Bleeding from a large
    vessel can kill in less time than lack of air
    ([Hospital Corpsman, p. 4-1](../library/navedtra-14295-hospital-corpsman.html)).
    See [Bleeding and wounds](bleeding-and-wounds.html).
4. **Airway.** Speak to them and tap the shoulders. If there is no answer,
    open the airway: tilt the head back and lift the chin. If you suspect a
    neck injury, move the head as little as possible
    ([Wikipedia, 'Cardiopulmonary resuscitation'](../library/wikipedia-en.html)).
5. **Breathing.** Look and listen for normal breathing, for no more than
    10 seconds ([Wikipedia, 'First aid'](../library/wikipedia-en.html)).
    - **Not breathing, or only gasping:** have someone send a
      [Mayday](../emergency/mayday-distress-call.html) now and fetch the
      defibrillator (AED) if you carry one. Start CPR. Push hard and fast in the
      middle of the chest, 5 to 6 cm (2 to 2.4 in) deep, 100 to 120 times a
      minute; give 2 rescue breaths after every 30 compressions
      ([Wikipedia, 'Cardiopulmonary resuscitation'](../library/wikipedia-en.html)).
      Use the AED as soon as it arrives and follow its voice instructions
      ([Wikipedia, 'Cardiopulmonary resuscitation'](../library/wikipedia-en.html)).
      For a person pulled from the water, start with rescue breaths: see
      [Near drowning](near-drowning.html).
    - **Breathing but unconscious:** turn them on their side, head low, so the
      tongue and vomit cannot block the throat
      ([Wikipedia, 'Recovery position'](../library/wikipedia-en.html);
      [Hospital Corpsman, p. 4-2](../library/navedtra-14295-hospital-corpsman.html)).
      Never give an unconscious person anything by mouth.
6. **Circulation and shock.** Look for pale, cold, sweaty skin and a fast,
    weak pulse. Lay them flat, keep them warm, handle gently
    ([Hospital Corpsman, p. 4-24](../library/navedtra-14295-hospital-corpsman.html)).
7. **Disability.** Note whether they are alert, confused or only respond to
    pain, and whether they can move all four limbs.
8. **Expose and examine.** Cut clothing along the seams to look for hidden
    injuries; then cover them again and keep them warm
    ([Hospital Corpsman, pp. 4-2, 4-4](../library/navedtra-14295-hospital-corpsman.html)).
9. **Call a doctor by radio** as soon as the first steps are done, or at
    once if you have a helper. See
    [Getting medical advice by radio](getting-medical-advice-by-radio.html).
10. **Write it down.** Time, what happened, what you found, what you did.

## Why

Brain damage begins within 4 to 6 minutes when breathing stops, and bleeding
from a cut artery can kill even faster, so these come first
([Hospital Corpsman, p. 4-1](../library/navedtra-14295-hospital-corpsman.html)).
Moving a person with a broken neck, back or ribs can make the injury much
worse; examine them where they lie unless the place is dangerous
([Hospital Corpsman, p. 4-1](../library/navedtra-14295-hospital-corpsman.html)).
Anyone with a head or face injury, or pain in the neck or back after a fall,
should be treated as having a spinal injury
([Hospital Corpsman, pp. 4-51 to 4-52](../library/navedtra-14295-hospital-corpsman.html)).
At sea there is no ambulance, so the doctor's advice by radio is your
back-up; the US Public Health Service guide says the ship's first-aider is
"no longer an independent practitioner" for serious cases
([PHS, p. xiv](../library/phs-ships-medicine-chest.html)).

## Check that it worked

- The person is breathing and the airway stays clear.
- Bleeding has stopped or is slowing under the dressing.
- Pulse, breathing and level of response are written down every 15 minutes
  ([Hospital Corpsman, p. 4-24](../library/navedtra-14295-hospital-corpsman.html)).
- A doctor has been consulted, and you have the advice in writing.

## If that fails

- **No signs of life after CPR has started:** keep going until help takes
  over, you are exhausted, or a doctor tells you to stop. Take turns if there
  is more than one rescuer. A cold person must not be declared dead until
  warmed ([PHS, p. 1-18](../library/phs-ships-medicine-chest.html)): see
  [Hypothermia](hypothermia.html).
- **You cannot reach a doctor and the person is getting worse:** send a
  [Mayday](../emergency/mayday-distress-call.html) and head for the nearest
  help.
- **Several casualties:** first check each one only for airway, breathing and
  circulation, and treat those problems before anything else
  ([Hospital Corpsman, p. 4-1, rule 3](../library/navedtra-14295-hospital-corpsman.html)).
