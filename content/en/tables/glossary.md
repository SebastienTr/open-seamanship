Words a crew of mixed languages need when things go wrong, or when reading a
French chart, forecast or harbour guide. English is on the left. Where French
has two common words, both are given. For wind force and sea state names in
French, see [Beaufort scale](beaufort-scale.html) and
[Sea state](sea-state.html).

Sources: French Wikipedia's "Glossaire maritime" and "Glossaire de la
navigation à voile", the English Wikipedia "Glossary of nautical terms", and
the French links of the English Wikipedia article for each term
([wikipedia-fr](../library/wikipedia-fr.html),
[wikipedia-en](../library/wikipedia-en.html)).
