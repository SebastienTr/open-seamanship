You may copy, share and adapt this pack, including commercially, as long as you
keep the credits and licences.

- **Our own pages** are under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
  licence (CC BY-SA 4.0).
- **Library documents** keep their own licence: most are public domain (US
  federal works, old books), others are under the UK Open Government Licence,
  the French Licence Ouverte 2.0 or CC BY 4.0. Each page's footer says which.
- **Wikipedia articles** are CC BY-SA 4.0; images keep their own licence.

Every page lists its sources. The full list, with where each file came from and
how its licence was checked, is in `sources.yaml` in the project repository.

This pack does not replace training, official publications or local rules.
