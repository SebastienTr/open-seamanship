If a page is wrong, unclear or dangerous, please report it when you are back
online, by opening an issue in the project repository.

Include:

1. The page title.
2. The pack version, shown at the bottom of every page.
3. What is wrong, and your source if you have one.

Reports about emergency, medical, gas, electrical or navigation pages are
treated first, and block the next release until they are fixed.
