## Finding a page

- **Something is going wrong now**: open **Emergency** from the home page.
- **Something broke**: open **Fix it** and pick the system, or search for the
  symptom ("engine won't start", "battery not charging").
- **Learning or planning**: use the sections, then follow links into the
  library for the full manuals.

## Review labels

Each page says how far it has been checked:

- **Reviewed**: checked against its sources by people who know the subject.
- **Verified**: reviewed, and tried in practice.
- **Unreviewed**: a draft. Only preview builds contain drafts. Do not rely on
  them.

An **Uncertain** box marks a point the sources do not settle. Use your own
judgement there.

## Where a page applies

Pages marked **International** apply everywhere. Regional pages (for example
**France: Brittany** or **United Kingdom**) apply only there; rules and rescue
services differ between countries.

## Saving power

Pages are plain text with no scripts, so they load fast and work in any reader.
Turn screen brightness down and use your reader's dark mode at night. Print the
emergency cards before you need them.
