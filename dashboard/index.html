Mayday call, word for word
  1. DSC first, if you have it: lift the cover, hold the red DISTRESS button until the radio sends (about 5 seconds).
  2. Channel 16, high power: «MAYDAY, MAYDAY, MAYDAY. This is [boat name ×3], call sign […], MMSI […].»
  3. «MAYDAY [boat name], call sign, MMSI.»
  4. «My position is [latitude, longitude, or bearing and distance from a landmark].»
  5. «[What is happening: sinking, fire, man overboard…]. I require immediate assistance.»
  6. «[Number of people on board, other useful facts]. Over.»
  7. No answer within about a minute: repeat. Keep listening on 16.

Draft, not yet reviewed. Format after ITU practice as taught in the US Coast Guard Boat Crew Seamanship Manual. In France the CROSS answers on VHF 16 and on 196 (mer.gouv.fr).
